How it works

Answer A short set of questions about the work you actually do, not the title you hold.
Map Your answers land on the Knowledge Quadrant, a framework for how knowledge work survives contact with AI.
See where you stand You get a persona, a plain-language read on your exposure, and what to strengthen next.

The honest fine print

Kairos runs on Claude (Anthropic's API), with a local-model fallback. No account, no login, and nothing is stored after your session ends.

It is in beta. Some answers will be rough. If something breaks or a question reads wrong, I want to know.

The framework behind it is written up in full at puneetsrivastava.com.
