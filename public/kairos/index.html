The idea behind it

The Knowledge Quadrant Framework

“The question nobody is asking clearly enough is not whether AI will take your job. It is which part of what you do is actually yours.”

The framework maps how people create value at work, and how exposed that value is to AI. Two axes. Knowledge: explicit knowledge is documented and searchable, the kind AI replicates at scale; tacit knowledge is judgment earned by living through real situations. Output: accuracy means there is a right answer and being wrong costs something; originality means there is no right answer, only a fresh one.

The Doctors

Explicit + Accuracy

Surgeons, lawyers, analysts, engineers writing production code. High stakes, verifiable output. AI is entering this room fastest.

Exposure: highest

The Builders

Explicit + Originality

Marketers, designers, copywriters, product communicators. Pattern-based creativity at scale. AI is displacing the volume tier first.

Exposure: high

The Generals

Tacit + Accuracy

Strategists, senior leaders, negotiators, advisors. Consequential calls on incomplete information. The human premium zone, and it is growing.

Exposure: lower

The Creators

Tacit + Originality

Work that comes from lived judgment and a point of view no one else holds. This quadrant creates history. AI can assist but cannot originate.

Exposure: lowest
How it works

Three steps, about five minutes

1. DescribeAnswer four short questions about today, or write it all in one go.
2. ReadKairos maps your day onto the four quadrants and eight personas, from The Doer to The Oracle.
3. ReflectSee the tasks a tool could handle, what gave you energy, and one honest question to sit with.
